WHY THIS PAGE EARNS ITS PLACE The card on the Integrations page answers "is this connection working". This page answers the question that follows: "what stops working if it is not". The dependants section is derived from the tool registry -- every registered tool that declares this integration -- and grouped by the AI employees that own those tools. So the page can say that four of the HR employee's capabilities are being simulated, which is the fact somebody needs in order to decide whether to go and find a credential. Everything below the configuration is the record of what this connection has actually done: the audit entries, the actions that queued for approval through it, and the execution records those approvals produced. CalendarEvent and ExternalIssue carry no integration column of their own, so they are reached through the approved action that created them. That is the only honest link available and the view says so. The configuration form here is inline rather than in a dialog: on a page about one connection there is nothing to dismiss it back to. It carries the same data-config-form contract as the dialogs on the Integrations page, so static/js/integrations.js handles both with one submit handler. No secret value appears anywhere below.
