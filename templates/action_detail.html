WHY THIS PAGE IS SHAPED THE WAY IT IS ------------------------------------- Three separate acts are offered and they are never merged into one button: Save edits writes the payload and leaves it PENDING Approve and execute carries it out Reject refuses it, with a mandatory reason A screen whose single obvious button both rewrote an employee's words and sent them would not be a review screen. So "Save edits" says in words, beside itself, that it does not approve; and an action whose risk is 'high' asks for a confirmation before it goes, because a one-click send to a customer is not a review. STRUCTURE left column the editable payload, then the read-only argument set, then the before-and-after comparison right column the decision panel, the execution outcome, the provenance, and the full audit trail The comparison exists because the difference between what an employee wrote and what was actually sent must always be recoverable.
