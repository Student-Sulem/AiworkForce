This is the company profile your AI employees read

Every employee consults these values before it drafts an email, writes a caption or schedules a meeting: the company name that signs a message, the tone of voice a post is written in, the hours a meeting may be booked inside, and whether a piece of work waits for a person. That is why the company name and the tone live here rather than in code -- changing how the whole workforce behaves should be a form somebody fills in, not an edit to a Python file followed by a restart.

Each setting is saved on its own and recorded in the audit trail with who changed it and when. Where the platform's approval policy applies to a change, saving queues it for review and says so instead of claiming it was applied.
